Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our pricing and plans.

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and billing is prorated.

What happens if I exceed my link limit?

On the Free plan, you'll need to upgrade. On paid plans, we'll notify you and provide options to upgrade or purchase additional capacity.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes! Annual billing comes with a 20% discount. Contact us for annual pricing details.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial for all paid plans. No credit card required to start.

Can I use my own domain?

Custom domains are available on Premium and Enterprise plans. You can use your own branded short domain.

What kind of support do you provide?

Free users get community support. Premium users get priority email support. Enterprise users get 24/7 phone and email support.
