7 Best URL Shorteners in 2026: Ranked and Reviewed

Malin Jayaweera·March 25, 2026·Updated March 28, 2026·10 min read

URL shorteners have evolved from simple redirect tools into full marketing analytics platforms. The best ones in 2026 offer real-time click data, custom branded domains, QR code generation, A/B testing, and — increasingly — AI-powered insights. We tested seven of the most popular options across these dimensions and ranked them for different use cases.

1. Trunc — Best Overall for Analytics and AI Features

Trunc earns the top spot by delivering enterprise-grade analytics on a free plan and offering features that no competitor has matched: AI Insights and A/B link testing. AI Insights analyses your click data and surfaces actionable recommendations — the best times to share links, which audience segments are clicking, and which campaigns are underperforming. A/B testing lets you send 50% of clicks to one URL and 50% to another, so you can measure which landing page, offer, or headline actually converts better.

  • Free plan: 10 links/month with full analytics (geographic, device, referrer, real-time)
  • AI Insights: automatic pattern analysis across your links
  • A/B testing: split traffic between two destinations
  • QR codes included on all plans
  • Custom domains on Premium
  • Team collaboration and API access

2. Bitly — Best for Integration Ecosystem

Bitly remains the most widely integrated URL shortener, with native connectors for HubSpot, Hootsuite, Salesforce, Zapier, and hundreds of other tools. If your stack already revolves around these platforms, Bitly's deep integrations reduce friction. The downside is cost — meaningful analytics and custom domains are locked behind plans starting at $29-199/month. For analytics depth alone, Trunc delivers more at a lower price point.

3. Rebrandly — Best for Custom Domain Branding

Rebrandly is purpose-built for branded short domains. Every link uses your own domain name — go.yourcompany.com/sale — which increases click-through rates by up to 39% compared to generic short domains. The trade-off is that Rebrandly's analytics are shallower than Trunc or Bitly, and the free plan limits you to 10 links with one custom domain. Plans start at $13/month.

4. Short.io — Best for Developers and API Users

Short.io is developer-first: its REST API is well-documented, supports webhooks, and has native SDKs for JavaScript, Python, PHP, and Ruby. Custom domains are included from the first paid tier ($20/month). Analytics are solid but lack the depth of Trunc's AI-powered dashboard. Best suited to developers who need to embed link shortening into their own applications.

5. TinyURL — Best for Occasional Personal Use

TinyURL is the simplest option: no account required, no analytics, unlimited free links. It has been operating since 2002 and has a proven reliability record. For personal use — sharing a link in a text message or a printed flyer — it does the job. For any business or marketing purpose, the lack of analytics makes it unsuitable.

6. T2M — Best for Lifetime Deals and Budget Teams

T2M (track-to-monetize) offers a one-time lifetime purchase option — you pay once and use the service forever. For small teams that want to avoid monthly subscriptions, this is an attractive model. Analytics and custom domains are included, though the interface is less polished than Trunc or Bitly and the AI features are absent.

7. BL.INK — Best for Enterprise and Compliance

BL.INK targets large enterprises with SOC 2 compliance, SSO, and detailed audit logs. If your organisation has strict security requirements — financial services, healthcare, government — BL.INK covers the compliance boxes that consumer-grade shorteners do not. Pricing reflects the enterprise focus, starting at $48/month.

How to Choose the Right URL Shortener

  • Need analytics on a free plan? → Trunc
  • Need AI insights and A/B testing? → Trunc
  • Already deep in the HubSpot/Salesforce ecosystem? → Bitly
  • Branding is the priority and analytics are secondary? → Rebrandly
  • Building a product that needs link shortening via API? → Short.io or Trunc
  • Occasional personal use, no analytics needed? → TinyURL
  • Enterprise with compliance requirements? → BL.INK

Final Verdict

For most marketers, developers, and small-to-medium businesses in 2026, Trunc offers the best balance of features, analytics depth, and price. Its free plan is genuinely useful — not a crippled trial — and features like AI Insights and A/B testing give it a material advantage over every competitor on this list.
