# Surface brief: flyer

## Scope
Product Showcase template (theme.style = flyer), rendered at /m/<slug>. Owner content replaces the sample content; owner theme tokens (accent, card_fill, text, button_text) drive colour.

## Visitor mode
Persuade. Small shops and sellers; the visitor spots a deal and taps Buy or WhatsApp.

## Constraints
Mobile-first, works without JavaScript, self-hosted fonts only with system fallbacks for non-Latin scripts, reduced motion respected, owner's brand leads (Trunc only in the removable badge).

## Direction contract
THESIS: the page sells like a printed weekly flyer; price and offer lead, not white cards.
OWN-WORLD: the owner's accent is a saturated field covering the header band; Barlow Condensed 800 headlines with huge price numerals; a starburst badge (clip-path, at most 12 points) carries badge text; a two-column dense product grid on white panels with thick accent rules; the carousel is a horizontal THIS WEEK strip.
STORY: spot the deal, see the price, tap Buy or WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: a masthead band with the shop name in condensed caps and a deal line; the first product is a hero tile with photo, huge price and badge; the grid starts within the first screen.
