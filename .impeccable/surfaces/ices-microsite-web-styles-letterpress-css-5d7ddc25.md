# Surface brief: letterpress

## Scope
Digital Business Card template (theme.style = letterpress), rendered at /m/<slug>. Owner content replaces the sample content; owner theme tokens (accent, card_fill, text, button_text) drive colour.

## Visitor mode
Persuade. Professionals and sales people met in person or by QR; the visitor saves the contact, then calls or messages.

## Constraints
Mobile-first, works without JavaScript, self-hosted fonts only with system fallbacks for non-Latin scripts, reduced motion respected, owner's brand leads (Trunc only in the removable badge).

## Direction contract
THESIS: the page is one thick card held in the hand, not a profile screen; it refuses the avatar-circle-on-gradient default.
OWN-WORLD: white card stock; the card's 6px edges painted in the owner's accent and visible as the page's frame; ground is the accent at 12%; the name large in Schibsted Grotesk 700 with a deboss (inset highlight and shadow, no glow); details in tracked small caps; no icon tiles, contact actions are typeset rows with small glyphs.
STORY: see who this is, trust it, save them in one tap, then call or message.
FIRST VIEWPORT: portrait card filling phone width with an 18px margin; logo or photo top-left, small and square; name about 40px over title and company; Save Contact is a full-width painted-edge bar at the card's foot; contact rows and socials below the fold on the card's back.
FORM: candidate 2 of 7 (impeccable's pick, chosen by the user over the assigned roll); seed:44559983ec2c413d
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
